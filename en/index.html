Life with Toshiko,
a gentle Shiba Inu walking game.

Pet her, play, decorate her room and go out for walks through a town that looks like your own, with neighbors, shrines and seasonal flowers along the way.
Nothing to fail and nothing to grind: just small, happy moments every day.

Every day with Toshiko

No tricky controls: everything works with one finger, at your own pace.

Pet, play and teach tricks

Pat her head and throw a ball. Draw a hand signal to teach her, and as your bond grows she learns Paw, Spin and more.

Walk your own neighborhood

Quiet houses, a shopping street, a riverside path: the town takes on the feel of where you live, through cherry blossoms, autumn leaves and snow, with small shrines and antique shops along the way.

Neighbors with stories

The baker, the judo teacher, the shrine maiden. Meet them again and again, and they open up and share their stories.

Dress up and decorate

A floral kimono, a summer yukata, even a dinosaur suit. Furnish her room with lamps, chairs and antiques.

Fortune Tail

Once a day, her tail tells your fortune, with a lucky color and a lucky thing to do.

Bath time and brushing

Lather her up, rinse her off and towel her dry, then brush her coat until it's fluffy.

A diary of memories

What she found, who you met, the little moments. Your days together are kept in her diary.

Gentle reminders

A quiet nudge for the morning walk or a treat. Easy to switch off in Settings.

A storybook town to stroll through

  • Cafés, bakeries, florists and bookstores, each with its own character.
  • Street trees, lantern lamps and flowers along the curb, with petals drifting by in season.
  • Spot a shining star on the ground and Toshiko trots over to dig up a treasure.

Kind by design

Made with older players and first-time gamers in mind.

  • No failing and no penalties. When she's tired, EXP simply takes a rest.
  • Login days never reset, so there's no streak pressure.
  • Big buttons, adjustable text size and VoiceOver support.
  • No loot boxes, and no countdown timers rushing you.
  • Purchases are optional and always confirmed first.
  • Fully in English and Japanese.

FAQ

Anything else? Just email us at the address below.

Is it free?

Yes, you can play the whole game for free. You can optionally buy Stars for cosmetic items, or join the SL Kennel Club (¥500 a month), which removes ads and gives you bonus Stars when you join and every month (the amounts are shown on the purchase screen). None of it changes how the game plays.

Are there ads?

The app may show ads, such as video or banner ads, and the kinds of ads may change over time. Joining the SL Kennel Club turns them off. Purchases are always optional and always confirmed before you pay.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone X and later, running iOS 16.0 or later.

Does it send my location?

Your exact location never leaves your phone. Only a rough area, rounded to about 1 km, is used to look up the weather and what your neighborhood looks like. See the privacy policy for details.

What happens to Toshiko if I change phones?

Make a transfer code in Settings → Transfer & backup. Enter it on your new iPhone and Toshiko comes over in full: personality, level, items and diary. You can also keep a backup file.
