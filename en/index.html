  • Purchases are optional and always confirmed first.
  • Fully in English and Japanese.

FAQ

Anything else? Just email us at the address below.

Is it free?

Yes, you can play the whole game for free. Optional purchases are cosmetic only (furniture, outfits) and never change how the game plays.

Are there ads?

The app may show ads, such as video or banner ads, and the kinds of ads may change over time. Purchases are always optional and always confirmed before you pay.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone X and later, running iOS 16.0 or later.

Does it send my location?

Your exact location never leaves your phone. Only a rough area, rounded to about 1 km, is used to look up the weather and what your neighbourhood looks like. See the privacy policy for details.

What happens to Toshiko if I change phones?

Make a transfer code in Settings → Transfer & backup. Enter it on your new iPhone and Toshiko comes over in full: personality, level, items and diary. You can also keep a backup file.
